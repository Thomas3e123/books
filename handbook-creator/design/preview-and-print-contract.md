# Handbook preview and print design contract — v1

## Progressive full-color preview
- On completing **each** interview section, show a prominent "View My Handbook So Far" action.
- The preview includes **every approved section through the current point**, in handbook order, with complete text, not summaries, excerpts, or sample pages.
- Show professional cover, title, table of contents (for completed sections), page headings, section dividers, and acknowledgment pages when available.
- Permit returning to questions, changing answers, and refreshing preview. Changed dependent policies must be flagged for reconciliation before final release.
- Use the same document model for previews and final exports. No misleading placeholder text presented as approved policy.
- Before purchase, full-color preview is readable and navigable but no normal text selection, clipboard copy, download, printing, or export. Do not promise to prevent screenshots, photography, OCR, or determined technical extraction.

## Premium document appearance
- A refined restaurant-appropriate cover with business name, effective date, understated green and gold accents, optional uploaded logo.
- Interior typography with strong readable hierarchy, spacious margins, consistent numbered sections, running headers, page numbers, and deliberate page breaks.
- Accessible contrast, sensible grayscale conversion, and printer-safe layout. Avoid expensive full-page ink fills by default.
- No internal Gravity development labels or draft markers in purchased final output, except legally necessary notices and unresolved issues clearly requiring owner review.

## Printing and exports (after verified payment)
- Present two explicit choices: **Print in Color** and **Print in Black & White**.
- The on-screen preview remains **full color** regardless of the chosen print setting.
- Color mode preserves designed accents and brand logo; monochrome mode uses genuine grayscale/black text, borders and backgrounds, not merely a printer setting.
- Render PDF with selectable color/grayscale output, then use native print dialog with corresponding mode; also support PDF export in both color and grayscale.
- Prevent all print/export routes before license verification; enforce entitlement in privileged desktop/backend handlers, not only by hiding buttons.
- Ensure print previews, table of contents, pagination, page breaks, cover and acknowledgments work in both modes.

## Acceptance checks
1. Approve section 1 -> view all of section 1 in full color.
2. Approve section 2 -> view sections 1 and 2 in full color.
3. Edit section 1 -> section 2 dependency status updates if needed; refreshed preview reflects edit.
4. Before payment -> cannot use built-in copy, print, or export controls.
5. After verified payment -> can print color or grayscale and export each PDF mode.
6. Full-color preview does not switch to grayscale when B&W printing is selected.
7. Long policy text flows across pages without clipped text, orphaned headings, or broken TOC.
8. Windows and web output use matching templates and section order.

Status: **design specification**, not implemented or tested.
