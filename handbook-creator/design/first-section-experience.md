# First-section conversion experience — highest-priority UX requirement

The customer's first tangible success must occur within approximately 60–90 seconds, not after completing a long setup. The first finished section is the product's most important proof of value.

## Opening flow
1. Start immediately with a short welcome: "See your handbook take shape in minutes."
2. Ask **one useful question at a time**, with Skip for Now on all non-legal-critical entries.
3. The first section should be **Welcome & About Our Restaurant**, not a burdensome legal/jurisdiction questionnaire. Ask optionally for company name, a short description/values, and a logo; offer sensible *editable example wording* explicitly labeled as a suggestion, never silently treated as owner-approved.
4. Show progress clearly, ideally no more than 3–4 short prompts before first preview.
5. Provide a primary button: **See My First Finished Section**. This should be the largest, most visually prominent action at section completion.
6. Immediately show the actual premium, full-color handbook cover and professionally typeset first section in a read-only preview. It must be usable with skipped optional fields and without payment, registration, or logo upload.
7. Show a clear **Edit This Section** action and a secondary **Continue Building My Handbook** action. Avoid a payment modal before the first preview.
8. Continue allowing a complete cumulative preview after every subsequent section, showing all approved sections to date.

## Quality and trust
- First-section preview should look like the final purchased output, including the same document layout, fonts, spacing, and cover; no low-quality temporary preview.
- If all inputs are skipped, render a truthful polished generic welcome heading and neutral content clearly identified as suggested draft; do not fabricate restaurant-specific details.
- Avoid placeholder brackets, empty headings, developer labels, or legal-compliance claims.
- Never force a logo, company name, account creation, or checkout before first preview.
- Preview is always full color; copying, printing, and exports remain gated by verified $29.99 license.
- Provide easy return to skipped questions; preserve drafts locally.

## Measurement (privacy-conscious)
Track only aggregate, consent-appropriate events if analytics are enabled: first section started, first preview opened, preview-to-next-section continuation, checkout initiated, purchase verified. No sensitive handbook answers in analytics.

## Acceptance checks
- New user can see first premium full-color section without providing a logo, company name, payment, or optional data.
- New user can see it in 60–90 seconds with minimal interaction.
- First preview matches eventual color export layout.
- First preview is a complete section, not a mockup.
- Navigation back and forward retains entered answers.
- Subsequent previews accumulate earlier sections.
- Legal-critical data is requested only when necessary for legally sensitive policies.
- First preview works on both Windows and private web build.
