Privacy

Information about this publishing website, direct contact and links to separate services.

Information you choose to send

If you contact Thomas Monroe Books by email, the information you provide is used to review and reply to your publishing inquiry. Do not send confidential employee, payroll or customer information to the general publisher email.

Website and hosting

This website is hosted on a third-party platform. Standard technical information, such as browser details, request data and usage patterns, may be processed for security, reliability and site improvement.

Optional digital book purchases

If you choose paid digital checkout, you are redirected to Square to complete payment. Square processes payment and receipt information under its own privacy terms; Thomas Monroe Books does not ask for card numbers on this site. For delivery, our website checks the Square order and payment status, including refunded amounts, before issuing a short-lived PDF download link. Order identifiers may appear in your checkout return URL; do not publish or share receipts or download links. Our hosting provider may retain security, access and error logs for normal operations. Contact us if you need help with a purchase or a privacy inquiry.

External destinations

Links to The Gravity Restaurant Leadership System lead to a separate website with its own registration and tool experiences. The Gravity website's privacy statement applies to those services. This publisher website does not provide Gravity employee accounts or cloud backups.

Questions

For a privacy inquiry about Thomas Monroe Books, use our contact page.
