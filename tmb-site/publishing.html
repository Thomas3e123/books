Home / The imprintThe imprint

A publishing home with a point of view.

Thomas Monroe Books brings thoughtful stories and practical resources into one collection, with publishing and software maintained as clearly different experiences.

What we publish

Writing informed by work.

Our list centers on leadership, responsibility, everyday working life and tools that help people follow through. Titles range from narrative books to structured restaurant management workbooks.

Every publication should reward the time a reader gives it. Some invite reflection; others are meant to remain close to the desk, the kitchen or the manager’s office.

What we stand for

Clarity without clutter.

Readers should be able to identify the book, its purpose and the author without navigating software dashboards. That is why this site is intentionally a publisher website rather than an operating platform.

Thomas Monroe Books is the publishing imprint. The Gravity Restaurant Leadership System is a separately presented destination for software, training and free digital operations resources.
