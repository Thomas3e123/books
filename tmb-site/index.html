The author

Behind the books is a lifetime of work.

Thomas Butler writes from more than three decades in restaurants—from the daily realities of service to the responsibilities of general management.

His books explore how people are treated, what good leaders notice, and how practical standards can bring clarity to difficult work. The result is a collection that moves between reflection and useful instruction.
