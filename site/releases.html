October 10, 2026 · V15.4

Gravity SOP Foundry — Hosted Employee Connection

Foundry now connects the browser-based manager workspace to a restaurant-specific hosted Employee App. Managers keep working drafts local while deliberately Published SOPs, Published recipes, Active posters, and employee-facing branding synchronize to the hosted workspace. Employees can open the same Employee App from phones and other internet-connected devices without sharing the manager computer or relying on same-Wi-Fi access.

  • Restaurant-coded employee link and QR with a separate Employee Library PIN.
  • The default 0000 PIN is blocked for hosted access.
  • Publishing, withdrawing, poster changes, and Employee PIN changes can synchronize to the hosted employee workspace.
  • Employee questions/error reports, training, acknowledgments, and learning activity flow back to the manager workspace.
  • Manager Inbox, Training Records, and Gravity Signals pull new hosted employee records when opened.
  • Working drafts, manager PINs, backups, and manager-only Gravity Signals are never sent to the employee workspace.
  • Gravity Signals is now explicitly optional: first-use Enable / Not Now choice, master switch, category switches, and individual Signal-type controls.
  • A manager-only Gravity Signal Interpretation Map explains possible system causes, possible people/training causes, what to verify next, and what not to assume.
October 10, 2026

Gravity V15.3 — Foundry Existing-SOP Import & Export

Gravity SOP Foundry Online now adds a dedicated Import / Export Center for restaurants that already have reliable SOPs. Customers can use Gravity masters, import their own procedures, create blank SOPs, or mix all three approaches. Imported SOPs enter as Drafts and never bypass manager review, approval, publication, or training decisions.

  • Direct conversion for Gravity transfer/JSON, CSV, TSV, TXT, RTF, HTML, DOCX, and XLSX.
  • ZIP/folder batch intake and copy/paste.
  • Review intake for PDF, legacy XLS, and common image formats.
  • Duplicate controls: Skip, Replace matching working copy, or Keep both.
  • Exports: Gravity package, JSON, CSV, TSV, Excel, Word, RTF, TXT, HTML, Print/PDF, and ZIP bundle.
  • Online/Desktop interface and workflow parity established as a release requirement.
