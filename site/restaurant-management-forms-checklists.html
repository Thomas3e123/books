Permanent Tool ID & Primary-Owner Crosswalk

Permanent IDs do not change when a restaurant customizes a form. The primary record owner below controls completion, handoff, and verification; supporting roles may perform assigned work without changing the form’s ownership.

Permanent IDControlled formPrimary record ownerSupporting / verification roles
GRV-OPS-PS-01Pre-Shift Checklist by StationManager on DutyStation leads complete assigned readiness checks; MOD verifies exceptions and handoffs.
GRV-MGR-LOG-01Manager Log & Shift HandoffManager on DutyIncoming manager reads/acknowledges; GM reviews recurring or unresolved items.
GRV-IV-CAR-01Corrective-Action RecordAssigned corrective-action ownerMOD/GM controls due dates; a verifier closes only after evidence confirms correction.
GRV-SVC-SW-01Server Side-Work ChecklistFOH shift leader / service managerAssigned servers complete work; closing manager verifies condition.
GRV-PREP-PAR-01Daily Prep Sheet & Par ListKitchen manager / prep leadAssigned prep owners execute; manager verifies shortages and incomplete work.
GRV-INV-PAR-01Inventory Count & Par WorksheetDesignated inventory ownerCounters record evidence; kitchen/GM review material variance and order decisions.
GRV-FC-WST-01Waste / Spill / Comp / Remake LogDesignated food-cost ownerMOD records daily events; weekly manager review assigns and verifies correction.
GRV-TRN-SO-01Employee Training Sign-OffDesignated trainerEmployee demonstrates performance; manager or approved verifier confirms certification/recheck.
GRV-RC-MNT-01Equipment Maintenance LogDesignated facilities/equipment ownerMOD applies safe temporary control; GM/vendor/qualified verifier confirms return to service.

Small restaurants may assign several owner roles to the same manager. The control requirement is one clearly named accountable owner per record—not a new job title.
