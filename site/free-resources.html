Gravity Sales Intelligence & Forecasting System

Understand what drove yesterday's sales, forecast what is likely to happen next, and calculate the labor hours required to serve the demand.

  • Search prior car shows, festivals, parades, school events, weekdays, weekends, temperatures, and rain.
  • Track fiscal-year-to-date sales, guests, average check, labor, sales per labor hour (SPLH), rolling trends, forecast error, and repeated over- or under-forecasting.
  • Convert forecast sales, guests, minimum coverage, fixed work, and event workload into recommended labor hours.

How to use the resources

Review the language, structure, and forms. The goal is to see how the system turns a repeated restaurant problem into a standard that can be taught, measured, and followed up.

What to look for

  • Clear manager purpose statements.
  • Practical forms and review pages.
  • Language that supports fair accountability.
  • Tools that help the restaurant stop relying on memory.
