    START HERE

    How to get useful answers from AI
    The quality of an AI answer depends heavily on the quality of the information you give it. These prompts
    are written to make you supply the context, evidence, goal, limits, and output format that a real manager
    needs.

         GRAVITY AI
         PROMPT
         FORMULA

1        CONTEXT             What restaurant, role, station, shift, or problem are we dealing with?

2        EVIDENCE            What numbers, records, observations, or verified facts do you actually have?

3        GOAL                What decision, plan, checklist, conversation, or analysis do you need?

4        LIMITS              What must the answer not assume, change, disclose, or decide?

5        OUTPUT              What format would make the answer immediately usable?

    BEFORE YOU PASTE ANYTHING INTO AI
       Do not paste passwords, payment-card data, banking information, confidential login credentials, or
        other secrets.
       Remove unnecessary guest and employee personal information. Use roles or initials when the identity
        is not needed.
       AI can make confident mistakes. Verify calculations, policies, schedules, food-safety requirements,
        employment requirements, and other high-stakes information against the real source.
       Use AI to organize, analyze, draft, compare, and prepare. The restaurant leader remains responsible for
        the decision and the standard.

    FASTEST WAY TO USE THIS GUIDE
    Choose the prompt closest to the problem. Replace every [bracketed field] with real information. Delete any
    section you do not need. Then review the answer against your actual records and standards before using it.

SHIFT LEADERSHIP

PROMPT 01 • PRE-SHIFT PRIORITY PLAN

 Act as an experienced full-service restaurant manager. Help me build a focused pre-shift plan for today.

 Restaurant context: [type of restaurant / service style]
 Expected sales or covers: [amount]
 Staffing: [FOH / BOH staffing and any call-outs]
 Known risks today: [large parties, weather, events, equipment, new employees, low inventory, etc.]
 Yesterday's problems that may repeat: [list]

 Create a concise pre-shift plan with: 1) the 3 most important outcomes for the shift, 2) the top risks to prevent, 3) what each
 manager or key position must verify before opening, 4) 3 talking points for pre-shift, and 5) what evidence I should review
 after the shift to know whether the plan worked. Keep it practical enough to use in a 5-minute manager huddle.

Manager check: Use the result to lead the shift - not to create more paperwork.

BEFORE YOU ACT ON AN AI ANSWER
      Can I point to the actual record, policy, standard, or observation behind this recommendation?
      Did the AI clearly identify what it does not know, or did it fill in missing facts?
      Does this decision affect food safety, employment, injury, insurance, banking, privacy, or law in a way that requires
       qualified review?
      Would I make the same decision if the AI had not suggested it?
      What evidence will tell me whether this action actually worked?

Gravity rule: AI may help prepare the decision. The manager still owns the truth, the standard, and the
follow-through.

GRAVITY
AI should make the manager clearer - not make the manager
disappear.
The first 50 prompts establish the core method. Every later expansion follows the same Gravity approach:
connect the question to real evidence, an accountable owner, a clear standard, and verified follow-through.

TRUTH
  Give AI the facts you actually know. Separate evidence from assumptions.

COST
  Ask what the unresolved problem is costing in money, time, safety, guest trust, employee stability, or
  management attention.

SYSTEM
  Turn the answer into a repeatable standard, tool, owner, review date, and verification step.
