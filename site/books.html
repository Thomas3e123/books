Leadership is unclear.

Start with Quiet Gravity and The Owner's Chair. Add the employee standards workbook when the restaurant is ready to turn leadership expectations into daily conduct standards.

Start with Employee Training & SOP Tracker and Restaurant SOP & Station Operations Builder. These tools help managers teach one clear standard instead of relying on memory.

Use labor control, Systems of Service, food cost, guest recovery, and weekly-review tools to help managers prepare, measure, and follow through during real restaurant pressure.

People and policies need consistency.

Use hiring, manager training, employee policy standards, and corrective-action tools to make expectations fair, documented, and teachable.
