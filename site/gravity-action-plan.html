Let’s make your first plan

Answer one question at a time. Examples show what to type.

What will this do?

Describe one problem that keeps happening at your restaurant. Gravity will prepare a simple numbered list of what to do.

You will review and edit the draft before using it. Nothing is assigned or sent to employees.

Practice only. The example is fictional. You can change every answer.

What keeps going wrong?

Choose the closest area, then describe what happens. Write it as you would explain it to another manager.

Example: Opening staff found unfinished closing work three mornings this week.

Who will lead the correction?

This is the person or role responsible for following the plan and checking progress. A job title is enough.

Example: Closing manager. If you will lead it, type “Me — manager.”

What have you actually seen?

Describe observations or records you can confirm. This gives you something to compare with later. Leave it blank if you have not measured it yet.

Example: On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, the opener found two closing tasks unfinished. Do not guess a number.

What should happen instead?

Describe the result you want in plain language. You do not need a percentage or a financial target.

Example: Closing tasks are finished, or the next manager receives a clear handoff for anything left open.

What could make this difficult?

Mention anything the manager should consider, such as equipment problems, a busy shift, unclear instructions, or training needs. Skip this if nothing comes to mind.

Example: Two new closers have not practiced the closing routine yet.

When will you start?

Choose a start date and a pace your team can manage. These choices set review dates; they do not create reminders.

60 days: more time for learning and practice. Check and correct at Day 30; make the final review at Day 60.

How to check your draft

  1. Check the problem and goal. Do they describe what you want to fix?
  2. Read the steps and training guide. Can your team follow them? Click outlined text to correct it.
  3. Check responsibility and dates. Are they realistic? Settle the unanswered questions against your approved procedures.

The working-checklist boxes record work actually completed later. Leave them unchecked during this first review.

Then save your draft. “Save on this device” keeps it in this browser. “Download working plan” gives you a readable text file. “Download backup” makes a file you can restore later.

Later: record what happened

Return after trying the plan. Record results and choose whether to keep it, change it, stop using it, or extend it. Leave the decision pending for now.

Decisions and notes save only when you choose Save on this device or download a backup.

Device storage can be cleared and is accessible to other people using this browser profile. No cloud synchronization or automatic reminders.

Built on Gravity’s working principles

One real problem. One accountable owner. Start with three working aids. Train before requiring records. Verify closure with evidence.

For problems outside these five areas, use Gravity Guide through the site’s Ask Gravity Guide button, or visit Start Here. Report confusing or incorrect output in the Testing Room; use Contact if it is unavailable. Use fictional or sanitized examples.
