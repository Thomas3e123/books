Gravity Handbook Creator: Android App Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Provided by Thomas Butler / Thomas Monroe Books, the publisher of The Gravity System.

This policy covers the paid Google Play Android app, package com.gravity.handbook. The Gravity website and private test apps are separate services.

Your handbooks stay on your device

The app stores your answers, business details, logo, design choices and handbook projects in its private storage on your Android device. It generates PDF, Word and editable project-backup files on your device. It does not send your handbook content to us or to a remote document-generation service.

No app account, advertising or analytics

The app has no account sign-in, ads, analytics, tracking or developer-operated cloud sync. It does not request access to your location, contacts, phone, microphone or camera, and it does not request broad access to your device's files. The app itself does not request network access.

Files you choose

You may select a logo or project backup using Android's system file picker. When exporting, you choose the location and approve the save in Android's file picker. If you choose a cloud-backed storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Exported files are outside the app's private storage; protect them and delete them separately when no longer needed.

Google Play purchases and updates

Google Play handles the paid app purchase, delivery and updates. We do not receive your card details through this app. Selecting Check for updates opens Google Play or your browser; Google's data handling is covered by Google's policies. The app does not download or install replacement APKs from our website.

Support

Contact support at thomas.butler@gravityleadershipsystem.com. If you send an email, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Avoid attaching a handbook containing employee or other confidential information unless it is necessary for your support request. The app does not automatically attach your projects or device files to support messages.

Retention, backups and deletion

Projects remain in private device storage until you remove the app's data or uninstall it. To remove all locally saved projects, open Android Settings, select Gravity Handbook Creator, then Storage, and clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Save an editable project backup first if you want to retain your work. The app disables Android automatic backup and device-transfer backup; save your own project backups. Removing app data does not delete PDF, Word or backup files you exported elsewhere.

Audience and policy changes

The app is designed for adults creating workplace policies, not for children. If app data handling changes, this policy will be updated and any required disclosures will be provided.
