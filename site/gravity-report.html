Founding editor’s note · October 20, 2026

What you can expect from The Gravity Report

Restaurants do not improve because someone gives another speech about standards. They improve when management identifies what actually happened, makes a defensible decision, and checks whether the correction holds. That is the purpose of this series.

Each issue begins with a question a restaurant employee, shift leader, manager, or owner might ask on a difficult day. We will separate symptoms from causes, examine the available evidence, suggest one manageable action, and define how to verify the result. The writing is meant to be useful even if you never purchase or download anything.

A useful exercise before the first issue: Write down one operating problem that has returned more than twice this month. Identify the record that could establish what actually happened, the person responsible for reviewing it, and the date you will return to see whether the cause changed. That is a better start than a broad instruction to “do better.”

Five complete articles were released October 20, 2026. The remaining twenty-five complete articles are scheduled for Tuesdays and Fridays through January 19, 2027.

— Thomas Butler, The Gravity Restaurant Leadership System
