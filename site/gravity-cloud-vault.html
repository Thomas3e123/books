Customer data control

Gravity Account & Encrypted Cloud Backups

Use an optional, verified customer account for cloud backups of Shift Lab and Inventory Center. This is separate from the free-book registration and does not change download eligibility.

Understand the protection: Cloud backup is optional and must be explicitly enabled for each workspace. Backups are encrypted in your browser with a separate recovery passphrase. Gravity cannot recover that passphrase. Keep a downloadable backup too. Cloud backup runs only while a participating tool is open and unlocked, not when your computer is off.

What this does not do

This feature is not real-time collaboration or multi-user conflict merging. It does not back up SOP Foundry, Work Study Lab, recipes, or other workspaces yet. It does not change manager/employee permissions or publish any information to employee apps.

To recover on a different device, sign in to the same verified account, open the relevant tool, and supply the correct recovery passphrase. Never share account passwords, recovery passphrases, or manager PINs with employees.
