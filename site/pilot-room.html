Restaurant Improvement Pilot

Work on one restaurant problem at a time.

The pilot is building toward 50 contributors across tool testing and real restaurant improvement. Choose one tool or one recurring pressure point, then record what you use, what gets in the way, and what the evidence shows. A flat or negative result is useful information too.

Join the pilot

Use a work email you can access. The room creates a private return link for your plan and check-ins. Save or bookmark that link. You can choose not to include restaurant-specific figures or employee and guest information.

Pilot feedback helps improve Gravity. Participation does not promise a particular restaurant result or require a public testimonial. Read the 50-person pilot plan · Privacy details.

Step-by-step test protocols

Choose a Gravity tool or process. Follow the setup and test cases, then record Pass, Fail, Blocked, Not tested, or Not applicable for each case. Use fictional restaurant data. If an expected result is unclear, mark it Blocked or Not tested and explain what needs clarification.

Do not enter real employee, guest, payroll, payment, or exact financial records. If a test fails, save the report and use Report a problem to describe the failure.
