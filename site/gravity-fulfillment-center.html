Universal Restaurant Safety & Reference Posters

Reference tools nearly every restaurant can use.

Use these as restaurant-specific posted controls, then confirm regulated instructions against your local code and approved procedures. Ordinary operational posters remain black and white; color is used only when it improves recognition or is itself part of the instruction.

Cooling Time & Temperature

Reference poster for the FDA Food Code cooling model: 135°F to 70°F within 2 hours and 135°F to 41°F within 6 total hours. Verify local adoption.

Hot & Cold Holding

Posted reminder for the FDA Food Code model holding limits: cold food at 41°F or below and hot food at 135°F or above. Verify local requirements.

Customize Holding Poster

Cross-Contamination Prevention

Keep raw animal foods separated from produce and ready-to-eat food; use dedicated/clean tools and prevent contaminated surfaces from contacting ready-to-eat food.

Customize Separation Poster

Emergency Contacts & Shutoffs

Restaurant-specific red-accent reference for emergency numbers, utility shutoffs, alarm information, evacuation meeting point, and manager escalation.

Customize Emergency Poster

Training Assignment & Verification Records

Record what was taught, check understanding, watch the employee perform the task, and document the result. Use one record per person and task. Keep your original record when follow-up training is needed.

Fast Record

A one-page editable Word form for routine station training. Includes restaurant and store number, the source and revision, understanding check, observed task, evidence, verifier decision, and retraining reference.

Download Fast Record — Word

Full Record

A four-page editable Word form for detailed assignments and follow-up. Includes attempt history, coaching, observation, evidence, approval, corrective-action links, and field guidance.

Download Full Record — Word

How to use either record

  1. Save a separate copy for the employee and enter the current lesson or SOP reference.
  2. Record prerequisites and the date or shift when training is due.
  3. Ask the employee to explain the task, then observe them carrying it out.
  4. Record the evidence and verifier decision. Assign coaching or retraining when needed.

Type in the Word fields or print and complete them by hand. A completed lesson or signature alone does not establish verified ability. Keep completed employee records in your restaurant’s own secure records system.
