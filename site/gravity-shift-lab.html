Gravity Shift Lab

Build the schedule and availability calendar as one workspace. Carry employee profiles, requests, qualifications and privacy settings forward; control opening and closing labor, OFF/OPEN/CLOSE entries, overtime, labor %, coverage, schedule revisions and employee-safe QR publishing.

Shift Lab boundary: this is the weekly scheduling workspace. It does not replace payroll, timekeeping, legal scheduling requirements, or Gravity Aware. It helps the manager build and inspect the planned schedule before posting it.

Delegated scheduling — how privacy works
  1. Manager/owner: unlock Manager Pay Lock, enter or update pay rates and the protected labor settings, then lock the private information before handing scheduling over.
  2. Scheduler/shift leader: build the schedule normally. Scheduled hours, overtime warnings, labor %, availability, time off, section coverage, coverage candidates and labor targets still work.
  3. Hidden does not mean ignored: protected pay rates and any manager-labor allocation stay active in the background, so labor % remains accurate even when the scheduler cannot see the underlying pay information.
  4. Management labor: the manager chooses whether Management-section hourly labor is included or excluded and can add a protected weekly salaried-management allocation when appropriate.
  5. Employee contact privacy: the manager chooses whether delegated schedulers may see phone numbers in Find Coverage. Phone numbers never appear on posted schedules, QR schedules or employee downloads.
  6. Manager unlock required: changing private labor settings, viewing/changing individual rates, seeing raw estimated labor dollars, Manager Labor Print, manager CSV, and backup/restore.
Privacy note: Manager Pay Lock is for normal delegated scheduling on a shared device. It reduces casual exposure of private information; it is not payroll, account-level authentication, or enterprise device security.

Call-off & replacement help

Find Coverage

Choose the day, shift and section. Shift Lab searches qualified employees, checks their availability and time-off rules, ignores anyone already scheduled that day, and flags overtime/custom-hour risks. Pay rates remain private.

Employee schedule publishing

Publish Employee Schedule + QR

The QR contains an employee-safe snapshot of the published schedule: names, roles, scheduled times and sections only. It never contains pay rates, labor dollars, phone numbers or manager-only notes. Because the snapshot travels in the QR link itself, employees can open it from another phone without logging in.

For a two-week QR, save the next week first. Very large two-week schedules may need to be published as separate weekly QR codes to remain dependable when scanning.

Sections / Stations

Enter the work areas you schedule separately, one per line. These carry forward every week. Employees can have a home section plus additional qualified sections, and any individual shift can be assigned to a different qualified section.

Common examples: Management, Dish Pit, Prep, Cook Line, Pizza, Salad, Expo, Servers, Hosts, Bar.

Opening and closing labor rules

Posted Open / Close + Setup / Closing Work

Shift Lab understands restaurant schedule language. OFF is a valid zero-hour entry. OPEN-4p uses the posted opening time minus the before-open setup allowance. 5p-CLOSE uses the posted closing time plus the after-close work allowance. Standalone OPEN or CLOSE is kept as an assignment marker but is intentionally excluded from labor totals until a second time is added.
