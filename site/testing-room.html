A personal invitation from Thomas Butler · Gravity Founding Tester Program

Help me test Gravity before I call it finished.

You do not need to be a restaurant owner, a programmer, or a formal beta tester. If you use one program, notice one confusing page, find one broken thing, suggest one idea, or tell me about something you have always wished existed to make work easier, that helps.

Every interaction is appreciated. If you materially help improve a Gravity Windows beta, you receive the final version of that program at no charge after the Founding Year ends. The 10 people whose testing contributes the most receive lifetime access to Gravity's standard digital library at no charge. Rewards are based on useful contribution—not praise, message count, or simply signing up.
