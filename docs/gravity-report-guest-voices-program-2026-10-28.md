# The Gravity Report — Guest Voices & Editorial Collaborations
**Draft operating program · October 28, 2026**  
**Status:** Research and editorial workflow. No invitations sent; nobody has accepted or endorsed Gravity.  
**Publisher:** Thomas Monroe Books · Gravity-branded editorial review required

## Purpose

Invite respected restaurant operators, working GMs, hospitality teachers, credible researchers, and subject-matter specialists to add **original, verifiable, useful** reports to Gravity's growing readership. The program is a collaboration between independent voices, not an influencer-paid endorsement channel.

**A follower count earns someone a look; it never earns them publication.** Audience relevance, credible operating experience, evidence quality, practical tests, respectful treatment of employees, transparency, and originality matter more.

## The weekly publication promise

Publish **three articles per week total**, not three plus any guest articles:
- Tuesday: The Gravity Report — full, independently useful guidance.
- Wednesday: The Gravity Field Note / **Guest Voice** (rotate one invited guest report into this slot, initially at most monthly; preserve original Tuesday/Friday schedule).
- Friday: The Gravity Report — full, independently useful guidance.

**Subscribers get a maximum of one optional weekly digest.** Do not send one email per article. New guest contributions are included in the same digest; no extra promotional messages or forced subscriptions.

## Guest approval and release gate

Accept when a submission:
1. Is written or explicitly authorized by its named author, original or clearly licensed, with documented publication/republication rights and image permissions.
2. Teaches a real restaurant leadership/operations/hospitality question, relevant to an actual working shift or owner decision.
3. Distinguishes observed results, opinions, assumptions, examples, and evidence; supports material claims with credible citations or documented testing.
4. Includes a concrete **try-this**, what evidence to observe, safety/people constraints, and how to verify whether it worked.
5. Avoids exaggerated guarantees, invented metrics, defamatory allegations, confidential employee/patient/customer/payroll information, plagiarized or unlicensed material, and fake testimonials.
6. Respects staff dignity: training, clear standards, privacy and system design before blame; maintains human judgment for sensitive decisions.
7. Discloses commercial relationships, incentives, vendors and relevant potential conflicts. No undisclosed paid placement, affiliate sales pitch, or disguised sponsored content.
8. Keeps the author's own informed perspective, including respectful disagreement with Gravity, while satisfying factual and ethical standards.
9. Is edited for readability, mobile access, citations, listening through Gravity Reader where rights allow, and accessible social snippets. Authors must approve material edits and title/attribution.
10. Includes a **byline, author biography and author website/social link**, and a genuine, optional invitation to share after publication. Do not require or claim a share as a condition of publishing.
11. Is reviewed and approved before its release date; new guest content is **never automatically published** from a pitch form or email.
12. Is attributable, correctable, and retractable when needed. Record editorial changes, version, and corrections publicly when substantive.

**Permissions:** Obtain written approval for publishing, audio narration (AI Cedar/Marin disclosure), any headshot or image, permanent archive, limited promo excerpts, and social preview assets. Agree in writing on nonexclusive rights, edits, publication date, and whether the author can republish. Do not assume an author endorses Gravity software or claims.

**Quality rubric (0–5 per dimension):** operator relevance (25%), truth/evidence (25%), practical action and verification (20%), independent judgment and human dignity (15%), originality/clarity (10%), audience fit (5%). Reach is a separate distribution consideration and **cannot rescue a low-quality article**. Internal editorial acceptance threshold: 4/5 weighted, with no failing evidence/integrity item. Guest author approval remains necessary.

## Collaboration approach

**A personal invitation, not a mass pitch.** Reference one specific useful publication by the prospect, propose ONE precise article question and the likely value to independent operators. Describe Gravity accurately as a new, independent restaurant resource; never claim a large existing audience or that the person follows Gravity.

Offer either:
- An original 600–1,000 word guest article including an implementable test.
- A 20-minute interview with editorially approved highlights, transcript and AI narration **only with explicit approval**.
- A short expert roundtable on a specific operations question, with attributed, agreed quotations.
- A co-developed printable field worksheet attached to a practical article.

Give a named byline, short bio, link to their own work, high-quality social preview, reader-friendly page, and optional cross-promotion they can choose. Encourage authors to disagree when backed by evidence, instead of requiring Gravity promotion.

## Additional uses for The Gravity Report beyond articles

1. **Guest Voices** — carefully selected independent experts and owners, including thoughtful disagreement with Gravity.
2. **Operator Field Reports** — anonymized or consented, real test → what changed → what did not → what to test next. Never fabricate client cases.
3. **Ask the Operator** — reader-submitted questions with privacy-safe, researched answers.
4. **Gravity Field Notes** — one task, one measure, one shift, and a verification step.
5. **The Two-Sided Question** — respectful contrasting views, facts separated from debate.
6. **Benchmarks and explanatory data** — properly sourced, explicit methodology and applicability limits; never unsupported universal averages.
7. **Tools in Practice** — honest limitations and real use examples from Shift Lab, Work Study Lab, SOP Foundry and Inventory Center without sales copy.
8. **Quarterly Lessons** — index what readers actually tested and what remains uncertain; no inflated traction claims.
9. **Audio reports** in Gravity Reader — only released content, narrator disclosure, original author approval.
10. **Reusable educational sharing packs** — platform-specific intros, credited visual pull quotes and canonical Gravity article links.
11. **Topic collections** — onboarding, labor, guest recovery, inventory, leadership and management handoffs, with clear 'start here' pages for search visitors.
12. **Corrections and reader challenges** — correction log, criticism welcome, invite reproduction of a test.

## Guest issue publishing model

No external pitch creates an issue. Create a reviewed author record with permission flags, approved copy, release date, canonical URL, social preview, sources and correction policy. Enter it into a **separate guest editorial data source** rather than retrofitting the immutable original 30-article sequence. Published guest pieces appear in the Report index, RSS, sitemap and Gravity Reader only once released. Keep weekly three-article cadence and one digest; if a guest slot isn't ready, publish an approved original Field Note instead.
