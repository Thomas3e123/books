# Gravity Report: controlled Facebook and X publication queue
**Prepared October 29, 2026 · Operational draft — not posted or scheduled**

## Guardrails
- The website publishes its existing articles Tuesdays and Fridays; never change that schedule to suit social media.
- Pilot **two Facebook posts and two X posts per week** in separate time slots. If engagement is useful, a third post per channel may highlight a Field Sheet, but do not fill every day.
- Offer one email digest per week **only after** business-mail authentication, explicit consent, double opt-in, working unsubscribe and a sending service are verified. No newsletter sending is configured.
- Copy may refer only to already published, publicly accessible articles. Do not post unpublished Quiet Gravity drafts, future issues, or unverified case histories.
- One post should invite human recognition; one should provide operating value. Avoid repetitive ad copy, invented testimonials, promises of results, clickbait and employee shaming.
- No auto-posting API or social account permissions have been connected or verified. Share buttons and copy features are **manual assistance**, not scheduled posting.
- Editor checks each destination for 200 response, article release, correct canonical URL and working PDF before approving a post; do not publish on a failed check.
- Record publish time, platform, exact permalink, article, Field Sheet if present, and simple observations of reader questions. Do not invent engagement totals.

## First review-only batch (all source articles released October 20)
### Facebook — story / retention
Good employees do not always explain why they have stopped trusting a workplace. A resignation is often the first time management listens carefully enough to ask what changed. The Gravity Report looks at schedules, training, fairness and the promises employees were given. Read: https://gravityleadershipsystem.com/gravity-report/why-cant-i-keep-good-restaurant-employees

### X — operating / food cost
A high food-cost percentage is a signal, not a diagnosis. Check count units, invoices, purchasing, portions and sales mix before blaming the kitchen. Free article + prepared Field Sheet: https://gravityleadershipsystem.com/gravity-report/why-is-my-restaurant-food-cost-so-high

### Facebook — management follow-through
An explanation can be true and a task can still be unfinished. The useful question is what recovery decision was made, who owns the next step, and what will prove completion. Take the free Field Sheet into your next shift: https://gravityleadershipsystem.com/gravity-report/why-do-my-restaurant-managers-keep-making-excuses

### X — work standards
When a manager says a task is done, what counts as proof? One owner, a realistic deadline, a clear finish standard and a reviewer can replace repeated reminders. https://gravityleadershipsystem.com/gravity-report/how-do-i-get-restaurant-managers-to-follow-through
